The **Knowledge** area is where Notiky surfaces structured product context — decisions, documents, and connections — so you and your agents work from the same source of truth.

---

## What Knowledge is for

| You want to… | Use Knowledge to… |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Onboard a teammate | Show how decisions and docs connect |
| Prep an agent task | Ground assignments in approved context |
| Review product history | Find what changed and why |

---

## Use when

- You are a **PM or founder** preparing work for agents or humans
- You need to verify context before assigning a sensitive task
- You are connecting customer feedback to product decisions (with Conversations and Tasks)

## Start with

1. Open **Knowledge** from your workspace sidebar
2. Browse or search for the topic you care about
3. Link insights into a [Conversation](/conversations) or [Task](/tasks)
