We read every message. Use the channel that fits what you need.

## Documentation corrections

Found a typo, outdated screenshot, or confusing step in these guides?

Email [support@notiky.com](mailto:support@notiky.com) with:

- The page URL (for example, `/tasks` or `/cloud-quickstart`)
- What you expected vs what the page says
- Your workspace role (founder, PM, engineer) if it helps us rewrite the section

We update docs.notiky.com directly — no pull request required.

## Product feedback

Share ideas for the board, agents, conversations, or CLI:

- Email [support@notiky.com](mailto:support@notiky.com)
- Describe the workflow you are trying to improve and what you do today instead

Feature requests help us prioritize what ships next.

## Bugs and account issues

For something broken in the product:

1. Check [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) — most agent issues are runtime or daemon related
2. Email [support@notiky.com](mailto:support@notiky.com) with `notiky version` and `notiky daemon status --output json` (redact tokens)
